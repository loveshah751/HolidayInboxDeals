# Future Feature Ideas

1. **Smart filters & tags** – Let users filter by discount type (percent vs. dollar off), brand, category (fashion, electronics), or “expiring soon.”
2. **Favorite & pin** – Allow users to “star” offers they plan to use, and keep a pinned list at the top for quick access.
3. **Offer reminders** – Optional email or push notifications when an offer is about to expire, or a daily digest of new promos.
4. **Brand profiles** – Show small brand cards with logos, typical discount ranges, and links to past offers.
5. **Sharing & gifting** – One-click sharing of an offer via text/email, or a “Send this deal to a friend” button for gift inspiration.
6. **Savings tracker** – Let users log redeemed deals and estimate saved amounts, turning it into a “holiday savings scoreboard.”
7. **Deal insights** – Highlight trends (“Most common discount this week”, “Top brands sending deals to your inbox”).
8. **Dark/light themes** – Keep the Christmas theme but offer a toggle for users who prefer a simpler look.
9. **Onboarding & tutorial** – Short walkthrough showing how Gmail connect works and what data is (and isn’t) used.
10. **Mobile widget** – For mobile users, add a simple “Today’s best deals” widget or PWA shortcut.
